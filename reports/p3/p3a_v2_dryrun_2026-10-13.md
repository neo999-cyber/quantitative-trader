## Why the ladders are so few

The memo assumed "five weekly settlement cycles". Polymarket's short-dated BTC/ETH/SOL threshold markets are
the daily ladders — "Will the price of Bitcoin be above $76,000 on October 13?", ~45–60 markets a day across
the three assets, listed with **just under seven days** to run (listed 6 October, settled 13 October at noon ET). The
recorder admits a market only if its life from listing to settlement is at least 7 days (v1's horizon rule,
`MIN_HORIZON_DAYS` in `scripts/record_polymarket.py`), so these never qualify and are not recorded; the
`above` kind is absent from the catalogue for the same reason. Their volume on the active
list today: ~$80k on the settlement day, ~$12k the day before, ~$4k two days out, a few hundred dollars a
week out — the liquidity sits in the final 48 hours, inside the horizon the rule excludes.

What this does and does not say: nothing about the wedge (unread); the sample the registered rule can see is
too small to decide anything this year. The owner's options are in `docs/31` §16.
